Rightcharge is a modular infrastructure platform. You integrate selected modules via REST APIs, hosted journeys, or webhooks to deliver home-charging services for fleets. The platform handles the heavy lifting of driver onboarding, charger and vehicle data, electricity tariff costing, session ingestion, eligibility, reimbursement, and reporting, while you retain full ownership of the customer relationship.

## How partners interact

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="API-first">
    You build the driver-facing UI and orchestrate all flows by calling Rightcharge REST APIs directly. Best for partners with existing apps or portals who want full control over branding and UX.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Hosted journeys">
    You embed pre-built Rightcharge onboarding and management journeys via hosted URLs or iframes. Best for partners who want to go live quickly without building custom front ends.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Hybrid">
    You mix API calls for core data operations with hosted journeys for complex flows such as driver onboarding or tariff selection. This is the most common integration pattern.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Who the platform is for

Rightcharge serves a range of partner types, each integrating the modules that match their product and customer base.

- **Fleet platforms** add home-charging reimbursement and reporting to their fleet management software.
- **CPMS providers** extend their network with home charger enrolment and session ingestion.
- **Charger manufacturers** enable fleet programmes by connecting their hardware to tariff and reimbursement engines.
- **Leasing companies** offer home-charging benefits to drivers as part of their vehicle lease packages.
- **Energy suppliers** launch fleet energy programmes with accurate tariff-based costing and direct payment flows.
- **Vehicle OEMs** embed charging reimbursement into their driver apps and connected services.
- **Mobility service providers** manage home charging for ride-hail, delivery, and car-sharing fleets.
- **White-label platforms** resell Rightcharge capabilities under their own brand to sub-partners.

<Note>
  The platform is designed to complement, not replace, your existing systems. Rightcharge acts as the infrastructure layer; you own the customer relationship and front-end experience.
</Note>
