Energy payments let you settle home charging costs directly between the fleet operator and the energy supplier, so drivers do not need to pay their home electricity bill and wait for reimbursement. This reduces driver friction and opens new commercial models for energy suppliers who want to offer fleet-specific tariffs or billing arrangements.

<Info>
  Energy payments is a limited-availability feature. Contact Rightcharge to discuss eligibility and supplier partnerships before building this solution.
</Info>

## What this enables

- **Direct bill reconciliation**: the fleet operator receives a consolidated energy bill or payment instruction based on actual charging sessions, rather than reimbursing individual drivers.
- **Reduced driver friction**: drivers charge at home without worrying about cost recovery or submitting claims.
- **Supplier-side fleet programmes**: energy suppliers can launch branded fleet offerings with accurate, session-level billing.
