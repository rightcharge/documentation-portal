Solutions are the outcomes you launch for your customers. Each solution is composed of one or more Rightcharge modules, configured and combined to meet a specific business need. You can start with a single solution and expand over time as your product grows.

## Available solutions

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Home Charging Reimbursement" href="/solutions/home-charging-reimbursement">
    Reimburse fleet drivers for home charging based on their actual electricity tariff and policy rules.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Tariff-Based Costing" href="/solutions/tariff-based-costing">
    Calculate the exact electricity cost of every charging session using real tariff data.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Energy Payments" href="/solutions/energy-payments">
    Enable direct energy payments between fleet operators and energy suppliers, removing driver friction.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Fleet Reporting" href="/solutions/fleet-reporting">
    Access consolidated charging session data, cost summaries, and reimbursement totals for your fleet.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Solution matrix

| Solution | Key modules used | Partner types |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home Charging Reimbursement | Driver Onboarding, Session Ingestion, Tariff Engine, Eligibility, Reimbursement | Fleet platforms, leasing, CPMS |
| Tariff-Based Costing | Session Ingestion, Tariff Engine | Fleet platforms, CPMS, energy suppliers |
| Energy Payments | Tariff Engine, Reimbursement | Energy suppliers, leasing, fleet platforms |
| Fleet Reporting | Session Ingestion, Reimbursement | All partner types |

<Note>
  Solutions are not mutually exclusive. Most partners launch reimbursement first, then add costing and reporting as their product matures.
</Note>
