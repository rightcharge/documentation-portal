Rightcharge aggregates session, cost, and reimbursement data into a reporting layer you can access via API. Use this to build dashboards, run finance reconciliations, and provide fleet managers with visibility into home charging activity across their entire driver base.

## What you can report on

- Sessions per driver, charger, or vehicle over any time period
- Cost per session and total cost per period, broken down by tariff components
- Reimbursement totals and statuses
- Tariff breakdowns showing unit rates and standing charges applied
- Carbon emissions [PLACEHOLDER: confirm carbon reporting availability]
