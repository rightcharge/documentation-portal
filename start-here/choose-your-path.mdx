Rightcharge can be used as a complete platform or as individual modules that plug into an existing product. This page helps you choose the approach that matches your timeline, infrastructure and the product you want to launch.

## Which approach is right for you?

| Approach | Best for | Time to production | What you build | What Rightcharge provides |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full Platform | Partners launching a new home-charging product from scratch | Fastest | Your brand and UI | Everything: onboarding, charger and vehicle data, tariff calculation, reimbursement, reporting |
| Modular | Partners with existing infrastructure who need specific capabilities | Flexible | Your existing product plus an integration layer | Selected modules only |

Choose **Full Platform** if you want to go live quickly and do not have existing charging or energy infrastructure. Choose **Modular** if you already manage drivers, chargers or billing and need Rightcharge to fill specific gaps, such as tariff calculation or reimbursement.
