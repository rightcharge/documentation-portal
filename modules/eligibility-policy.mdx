The Eligibility & Policy module applies programme rules before a reimbursement is created. This ensures only eligible sessions from verified drivers are paid, and that your programme operates within defined cost and frequency limits.

## What you can configure

### Driver eligibility

Define which drivers qualify for reimbursement based on attributes such as role, vehicle type and employment status.

[PLACEHOLDER: full eligibility criteria options]

### Session policy

Set rules that individual sessions must pass before they are approved for reimbursement:

- Minimum and maximum kWh per session
- Session frequency caps (for example, maximum sessions per day or month)
- Home location verification (session must occur at the driver's registered address)

### Fraud controls

Apply automated checks to detect anomalous or duplicate activity:

- Duplicate session detection
- Anomaly thresholds (for example, unusually high kWh for the vehicle type)

[PLACEHOLDER: configurable fraud rules]

### Cost caps

Limit reimbursement exposure with per-session and per-period caps in GBP.

[PLACEHOLDER: confirm currency support]

## Configuration

Policy rules are configured per company or per fleet programme.

## Rejected sessions

Sessions that fail eligibility or policy checks are returned with a rejection reason. A webhook fires for each rejection so your platform can notify the driver or update internal records.
