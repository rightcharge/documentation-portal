The Driver Onboarding module manages the journey from driver invitation to a fully onboarded, verified profile with connected charger, vehicle and tariff. You can use Rightcharge's hosted journey or build your own UI and call the Rightcharge APIs directly.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Hosted Journey">
    Rightcharge provides a branded, embeddable onboarding URL. Your driver completes every step in browser without you building a custom UI.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Create a driver">
        Call the Rightcharge API to create a driver record. The response includes an `onboarding_url`.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Send the link">
        Deliver the `onboarding_url` to the driver via your preferred channel (email, SMS, in-app).
      </Step>
      <Step title="Driver completes steps">
        The driver follows the hosted journey: accepts the invitation, adds their home address, connects their charger, connects their vehicle and confirms their electricity tariff.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Receive webhook">
        Rightcharge fires a webhook when onboarding completes or fails. See [modules/webhooks-module](/modules/webhooks-module).
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API-driven">
    You build the UI and call each onboarding step via the Rightcharge API. This gives you full control over branding, sequencing and validation.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Accept invitation">
        The driver accepts the programme invitation via your UI. Your backend calls the Rightcharge API to record acceptance.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Add address">
        Collect and submit the driver's home address. This is used for home-location verification during session processing.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Connect charger">
        Submit charger details (make, model, serial number). Rightcharge attempts to link the charger to a supported integration.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Connect vehicle">
        Submit vehicle registration or VIN. Rightcharge attempts to link the vehicle to a supported telematics or OEM integration.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Confirm tariff">
        The driver selects or confirms their electricity tariff and supplier. This feeds the Tariff Engine for per-session costing.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Onboarding states

A driver moves through the following states during onboarding:

| State | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `pending_onboarding` | Driver record created; invitation sent but not yet accepted. |
| `onboarding_in_progress` | Driver has accepted and is completing steps. |
| `onboarding_complete` | All required steps finished; driver is eligible for session processing. |

## Data collected

The onboarding module collects the information required for downstream modules:

- **Home address**: used for home-location verification in the Eligibility & Policy module.
- **Charger details**: make, model and serial number; used by the Charger Integration module to retrieve session data.
- **Vehicle details**: registration number and VIN; used by the Vehicle Integration module for session attribution.
- **Electricity tariff and supplier**: unit rate, standing charge and time-of-use schedule; used by the Tariff Engine for cost calculation.

## Sandbox behaviour

In the sandbox environment, charger and vehicle verification steps are skipped. A synthetic tariff is applied automatically so you can test the full onboarding flow without live integrations.
