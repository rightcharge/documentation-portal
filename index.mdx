Integrate once. Deploy fleet home charging.

Rightcharge provides the infrastructure layer that fleet platforms, CPMS providers, charger manufacturers, leasing companies, energy suppliers, vehicle OEMs and mobility providers use to launch home-charging products for fleets. You add home charging reimbursement, tariff-based costing, energy payments and fleet reporting without building every charger and energy integration yourself.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Integrate Rightcharge modules">
    Connect the modules you need: driver onboarding, charger and vehicle data, tariff calculation, session ingestion, reimbursement and webhooks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch your product">
    Use hosted journeys or build your own UI on top of Rightcharge APIs. You control the brand and customer experience.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive lifecycle webhooks">
    Get real-time notifications as drivers onboard, chargers connect, sessions complete and reimbursements are processed.
  </Step>
</Steps>
